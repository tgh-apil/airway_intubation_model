## Skin

The following instructions detail the process for casting the silicone skin.

<img src="https://github.com/tgh-apil/airway_intubation_model/blob/main/images/skin_casting/0_skin.png" width="600">

### Materials & equipment
#### Hardware
- 4 x 40mm M4 screws
- 4 x 20mm M4 screws
- 4 x 10mm M4 screws
- 4 x 8mm M4 screws
- Power drill or screwdriver

#### Printing
- Hard plastic filament (PLA, etc.)
- Fused Deposition Modelling 3D printer

#### Casting
- Two-part Platinum Cure Silicone Rubber
  - Ecoflex 00-30: Available from [Smooth-On](https://www.smooth-on.com/products/ecoflex-00-30/)
- Silc Pig silicone pigments in skin tone of choice (medium flesh was used here): Available from [Smooth-On](https://www.smooth-on.com/products/silc-pig/)
- Mold release agent: Available from [Smooth-On](https://www.smooth-on.com/product-line/universal-mold-release/)
- Hot glue gun
- Mesh stretch fabric (nylon, polyester, etc.)
- Glue stick

#### Components
Attached components:
- forehead bar ([skin_forehead_bar.stl](https://github.com/tgh-apil/airway_intubation_model/blob/main/stl_files/skin_casting/skin_forehead_bar.stl))
- neck bar ([skin_neck_bar.stl](https://github.com/tgh-apil/airway_intubation_model/blob/main/stl_files/skin_casting/skin_neck_bar.stl))

Mold components:
- bottom ([skin_mold_bottom.stl](https://github.com/tgh-apil/airway_intubation_model/blob/main/stl_files/skin_casting/skin_mold/skin_mold_bottom.stl))
- inner ([skin_mold_inner.stl](https://github.com/tgh-apil/airway_intubation_model/blob/main/stl_files/skin_casting/skin_mold/skin_mold_inner.stl))
- outer left ([skin_mold_outer_L.stl](https://github.com/tgh-apil/airway_intubation_model/blob/main/stl_files/skin_casting/skin_mold/skin_mold_outer_L.stl))
- outer right ([skin_mold_outer_R.stl](https://github.com/tgh-apil/airway_intubation_model/blob/main/stl_files/skin_casting/skin_mold/skin_mold_outer_R.stl))

### Instructions
1. Print the components listed above.
2. Apply mold release agent to the mold components. Do not apply it to the forehead and neck bars, as the silicone is not intended to be removed from these component.
3. Cut out a rectangle of mesh fabric as per the image below–approximately 12cm x 8cm in size, with two small crosses cut out 5.5cm up the short axis and 1.7cm apart. Fit this piece of fabric over the inner mold component so that the holes fit over the nostrils and the mouth of the mold component is entirely covered by the fabric. A glue stick can be used here to help keep the fabric lie flat and unwrinkled against the inner component.
    <img src="https://github.com/tgh-apil/airway_intubation_model/blob/main/images/skin_casting/1_skin.png" width="500">
4. Optionally, in order to further reinforce the skin, you may choose to cut thin strips (about 1 cm or thinner in width and about 10 cm in length) of the mesh fabric and tie them around the cylindrical bridging bars inside the forehead and neck bars. This will prevent the silicone from tearing free from the forehead and neck bars once cast.
5. Assemble the mold.
    1. Attach the bottom mold component with four 10mm M4 screws.
    2. Put the forehead and neck bars onto the inner component. Ensure that the flat side of the forehead piece is facing “up”, toward the top of the head, and the flat side of the neck piece “down”, toward the neck. Do not attach with screws yet.
    3. With the inner component lying facing upwards, assemble the outer left and right sides, attaching them to each other with four 8mm M4 screws. Then attach to the forehead bar and inner mold component with four 20mm M4 screws, and to the neck bar and inner mold component with four 40mm M4 screws.
    <img src="https://github.com/tgh-apil/airway_intubation_model/blob/main/images/skin_casting/2_skin.png" width="500">
6. Mix 600 mL of Ecoflex 00-30, dyed with skin colour of choice (Silc Pig in the colour Medium Flesh was used here). Inject into mold using the largest vent hole.
7. Once cured, unscrew the outer and bottom components and remove them. Cut the injection path of the silicone at the bottom before removing the skin. Then carefully remove the skin and forehead and neck bars, cutting the remaining vent paths as you go. Once the skin has been removed, trim any flashing, cut open the mouth, and cut away the excess silicone occluding nasal passage. The mouth can be open slightly further if it is desired that the jaw should open wider.
