## Pathological trachea variant (tumor)

This pathological variant of the trachea represents a soft tumor that occludes vision into the airway but which can be pushed past with a scope. The soft silicone tumor is cast into a plastic shell which is attached around the trachea to hold the tumor in place; the tumor fits into the airway through a hole in the wall of the trachea. The following instructions detail the tumor casting and pathological trachea assembly process.

<img src="https://github.com/tgh-apil/airway_intubation_model/blob/main/images/trachea_tumor_casting/trach_tumor_view.png" width="400">

### Materials & equipment
#### Hardware
- 4 x 10mm M3 screws

#### Printing
- Hard plastic filament, (PLA, etc.)
- Flexible filament (TPE, etc.)
- Fused Deposition Modelling 3D printer

#### Casting
- Two-part Platinum Cure Silicone Rubber
  - Ecoflex 00-20: Available from [Smooth-On](https://www.smooth-on.com/products/ecoflex-00-20/)
- Degas chamber (optional)
- Silc Pig silicone pigments in blood and skin tone of choice: Available from [Smooth-On](https://www.smooth-on.com/products/silc-pig/)
- Mold release agent: Available from [Smooth-On](https://www.smooth-on.com/product-line/universal-mold-release/)
- Hot glue gun
- Clay

### Components
To be printed in hard plastic (PLA, etc.):
- Tumor mold ([trach_tumor_mold.stl](https://github.com/tgh-apil/airway_intubation_model/blob/main/stl_files/trachea/pathological/trach_tumor_mold.stl))
- Tumor holders parts 1 and 2 ([trach_tumor_holder_1.stl](https://github.com/tgh-apil/airway_intubation_model/blob/main/stl_files/trachea/pathological/trach_tumor_holder_1.stl) and [trach_tumor_holder_2.stl](https://github.com/tgh-apil/airway_intubation_model/blob/main/stl_files/trachea/pathological/trach_tumor_holder_2.stl))

To be printed in flexible plastic (TPE, etc.):
- Pathological trachea ([trachea_tumor.stl](https://github.com/tgh-apil/airway_intubation_model/blob/main/stl_files/trachea/pathological/trachea_tumor.stl))

### Instructions
1. Print the components listed above.

2. Apply mold release agent to trach_tumor_mold.stl. Do not apply it to trach_tumor_holder_1.stl, as the silicone is not intended to be removed from this component.

3. Assemble the two mold components with four 10mm M3 screws. Seal the gaps with clay, then use a heat gun to seal all cracks in the mold to ensure silicone does not leak out while casting.
<img src="https://github.com/tgh-apil/airway_intubation_model/blob/main/images/trachea_tumor_casting/trach_tumor_mold.png" width="600">

4. Mix 25 mL of Ecoflex 00-20 silicone, dyed with Silc Pig blood pigment. Inject into mold using the largest vent hole on the top of the mold.

5. Once cured, demold and remove trach_tumor_mold.stl. Do not remove the silicone from the holder component.
<img src="https://github.com/tgh-apil/airway_intubation_model/blob/main/images/trachea_tumor_casting/holder1_trachea.png" width="400">

6. Attach holders 1 and 2 around the pathological trachea so that the tumor fits through the hole in the trachea and is visible in the airway. Secure the two holders together using four 10mm M3 screws.
<img src="https://github.com/tgh-apil/airway_intubation_model/blob/main/images/trachea_tumor_casting/holder1_holder2.png" width="400">
