## Vocal folds

The following instructions detail the process for casting the folds. While the two variants—healthy and pathological (tumor)—require separate molds, different silicones, and a different connector component, the steps for casting both are the same.

### Materials & equipment
#### Hardware
- 4 x 12mm M4 screws
- Power drill or screwdriver

#### Printing
- Hard plastic filament (PLA, etc.)
- Fused Deposition Modelling 3D printer

#### Casting
- Two-part Platinum Cure Silicone Rubber
  - **Healthy anatomy:** Ecoflex 00-20: Available from [Smooth-On](https://www.smooth-on.com/products/ecoflex-00-20/)
  - **Pathological anatomy (tumor):** Dragon Skin 00-20: Available from [Smooth-On](https://www.smooth-on.com/products/dragon-skin-20/)
- Silc Pig silicone pigments in blood, white, and yellow: Available from [Smooth-On](https://www.smooth-on.com/products/silc-pig/)
- Mold release agent: Available from [Smooth-On](https://www.smooth-on.com/product-line/universal-mold-release/)
- Hot glue gun

### Instructions
1. Print the components listed above for the desired variant (the healthy variant is shown in the images below).
2. Apply mold release agent to the two mold components. Do not apply it to the wings, as the silicone is not intended to be removed from these.
3. Place the wings onto the bottom mold component so that the flat side of the wings faces upward, per the image below.

<img src="https://github.com/tgh-apil/airway_intubation_model/blob/main/images/vocal_folds_casting/2_vf.png" width="600">

4. Attach the top mold component with four 12mm M4 screws.
5. Mix 25 mL of Ecoflex 00-20 for the healthy variant, or 25 mL of Dragon Skin 00-20 for the pathological variant, dyed with Silc Pig pigments in the colours Blood, White, and Yellow until the desired colour is achieved. Inject into mold using one of the larger vent holes.
6. Once cured, demold the vocal folds, taking care not to remove the silicone from the wings. Trim any excess silicone from the vent holes or from flashing.
