## Mandible and tongue

The following instructions detail the process for casting the mandible and tongue, including both the healthy and pathological (tumor at base of tongue) variants. While the two variants require separate molds and different silicones, the steps for casting are the same.

<img src="https://github.com/tgh-apil/airway_intubation_model/blob/main/images/mandible_casting/0_mandible.png" width="600">

### Materials & equipment
#### Hardware
- 4 x 35mm M4 screws
- 2 x 25mm M4 screws
- 4 x 20mm M4 screws
- 4 x 18mm M4 screws
- 2 x 18mm M3 screws
- 6 x 14mm M3 screws
- Power drill or screwdriver

#### Printing
- Hard plastic filament (PLA, etc.)
- Fused Deposition Modelling 3D printer

#### Casting
- Two-part Platinum Cure Silicone Rubber
  - **Healthy anatomy:** Ecoflex 00-20: Available from [Smooth-On](https://www.smooth-on.com/products/ecoflex-00-20/)
  - **Pathological anatomy (tumor):** Ecoflex 00-30: Available from [Smooth-On](https://www.smooth-on.com/products/ecoflex-00-30/)
- Silc Pig silicone pigments in blood: Available from [Smooth-On](https://www.smooth-on.com/products/silc-pig/)
- Mold release agent: Available from [Smooth-On](https://www.smooth-on.com/product-line/universal-mold-release/)
- Hot glue gun
- Mesh stretch fabric (nylon, polyester, etc.)

### Instructions
1. Print the components listed above for the desired variant (the healthy variant is shown in the images below).
2. Apply mold release agent to the mold components. Do not apply it to the mandible or epiglottis casting plate, as the silicone is not intended to be removed from these component.
3. Optionally, cut out a small rectangle of mesh fabric large enough to cover the epiglottis plate—about 2cm x 4cm. Overlay it on top of the plate and use a marker to mark the location of the holes for the screws, then make small cuts in the fabric at these locations to ease the screw insertion process later. (Note: The mesh is not strictly necessary, but improves the longevity of the cast piece by preventing the epiglottis plate from tearing out of the silicone.)
4. Per the image below, align the epiglottis plate about 0.5cm above the bottom mold component so that the holes in the plate are aligned with those of the bottom mold component, and the concave side of the plate faces the convex side of the bottom mold component. Use two 14mm M3 screws to attach the two while maintaining the ~0.5cm gap. If using the mesh fabric, pass the screws through it as well and insert it between the epiglottis plate and the mold component.

<img src="https://github.com/tgh-apil/airway_intubation_model/blob/main/images/mandible_casting/2_mandible.png" width="400">

5. Assemble the rest of the mold.
    1. Align the body and ramus of the mandible with the groove in one side of the outer mold and attach the two with two 14mm M3 screws. Do the same with the other side of the outer mold.

       <img src="https://github.com/tgh-apil/airway_intubation_model/blob/main/images/mandible_casting/3_mandible.png" width="450">
    2. Attach the two side mold components with one 18mm M4 screw each in the following hole (see image). The side components will later also be attached to the back mold component with two 35mm M4s.

       <img src="https://github.com/tgh-apil/airway_intubation_model/blob/main/images/mandible_casting/4_mandible.png" width="450">
    3. Slide the bottom component (with epiglottis plate attached) into the slot in the back component so the curved portions at the fronts of the components lie flush.

       <img src="https://github.com/tgh-apil/airway_intubation_model/blob/main/images/mandible_casting/5_mandible.png" width="450">
    4. Attach the back component to the rest of the mold with four 35mm M4 screws (the ones that also pass through the side mold components), and two 18mm M4 screws.
    5. Attach the top mold component with two 25mm M4 screws and four 20mm M4 screws, then with two 18mm M3 screws to attach it to the mandible inside.

<img src="https://github.com/tgh-apil/airway_intubation_model/blob/main/images/mandible_casting/6_mandible.png" width="450">

6. Use a hot glue gun to seal the seams of the mold to prevent silicone from leaking during the casting process. Small pieces of paper towel may be crumpled into the deeper screw holes first to make the removal of the glue easier later on.

7. Mix 200 mL of Ecoflex 00-20 for the healthy variant, or 200 mL of Ecoflex 00-30 for the pathological variant, dyed with Silc Pig pigment in the colour Blood. Inject into mold using the largest vent hole.

8. Once cured, remove the hot glue and unscrew the mold components. Remove the mold components, cutting the vent hole paths as you go, until you are left with the back and bottom components. Before removing the silicone from these, you will need to unscrew the two 14mm M3s attached to the epiglottis plate which has now been embedded in the silicone. 

<img src="https://github.com/tgh-apil/airway_intubation_model/blob/main/images/mandible_casting/7_mandible.png" width="450">

9. Once the silicone has been fully removed from the mold, trim any flashing and cut away the excess silicone on the teeth to reveal them.
