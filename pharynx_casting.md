## Pharynx

The following instructions detail the process for casting the silicone component comprising the roof of mouth and back of throat.

<img src="https://github.com/tgh-apil/airway_intubation_model/blob/main/images/pharynx_casting/pharynx_cast.png" width="600">

### Materials & equipment
#### Hardware
- 46 x 10mm M4 screws
- 2 x 18mm M4 screws
- 2 x 20mm M4 screws
- Power drill or screwdriver

#### Printing
- Hard plastic filament (PLA, etc.)
- Fused Deposition Modelling 3D printer

#### Casting
- Two-part Platinum Cure Silicone Rubber
  - Ecoflex 00-30: Available from [Smooth-On](https://www.smooth-on.com/products/ecoflex-00-30/)
- Silc Pig silicone pigments in blood: Available from [Smooth-On](https://www.smooth-on.com/products/silc-pig/)
- Mold release agent: Available from [Smooth-On](https://www.smooth-on.com/product-line/universal-mold-release/)
- Hot glue gun

### Instructions
1. Print the components listed above in hard plastic filament.
2. Apply mold release agent to the mold components. Do not apply it to the pharynx, as the silicone is not intended to be removed from this component. It is also not necessary to apply it to the connector plates as they do not come into direct contact with the silicone.
3. Assemble the mold. (It is recommended to begin with one side; these instructions begin with the left for the sake of clarity, but beginning with the right is equally feasible.)
    1. Slot the back lower left component into the pharynx and attach together with an 18mm M4 screw.

       <img src="https://github.com/tgh-apil/airway_intubation_model/blob/main/images/pharynx_casting/1_pharynx.png" width="500">
    2. Slot the back upper left part A component into the pharynx and attach together with a 20mm M4 screw.

       <img src="https://github.com/tgh-apil/airway_intubation_model/blob/main/images/pharynx_casting/2_pharynx.png" width="500">
    3. Secure the back left components with the left connector plate and four 10mm M4s.

       <img src="https://github.com/tgh-apil/airway_intubation_model/blob/main/images/pharynx_casting/3_pharynx.png" width="350">
    4. Repeat the above steps with the right side of the back lower and upper components, and the right connector plate.
    5. Insert the upper front component, then the lower front component. Attach to mold with the left and right connector plates and six 10mm M4s.

       <img src="https://github.com/tgh-apil/airway_intubation_model/blob/main/images/pharynx_casting/5_pharynx.png" width="500">
    6. Insert the back upper left and right part B components.
    7. Assemble the three esophagus components (left, right, and inner) and attach to the main mold with the front and back lower connector plates and sixteen 10mm M4s.
       
       <img src="https://github.com/tgh-apil/airway_intubation_model/blob/main/images/pharynx_casting/7_pharynx.png" width="500">
    8. Secure the remainder of the mold with the remaining connector plates (back, back upper, and top) and sixteen 10mm M4s.

       <img src="https://github.com/tgh-apil/airway_intubation_model/blob/main/images/pharynx_casting/8_pharynx.png" width="350">
4. Use a hot glue gun to seal the seams of the mold to prevent silicone from leaking during the casting process.
5. Mix 300 mL of Ecoflex 00-30, dyed with Silc Pig blood pigment. Inject into mold using the largest vent hole.
6. Once cured, disassemble the mold in reverse order of assembly. Trim any flashing and cut out the teeth and airway.
